    https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/auxiliary/GraphemeBreakTest.txt

The segmentation rules follow UAX #29, Unicode Text Segmentation, revision 49
(Unicode 18.0.0): https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr29/

scripts/gen_ucd_tables.py turns the three property files into
src/bytetrunc/_ucd_data.py and src/bytetrunc/_ucd_part*.py. tests/test_ucd_tables.py
checks that the committed tables match a fresh run of the generator, and
tests/test_grapheme_conformance.py runs every case in GraphemeBreakTest.txt.
